# Privacy Policy for Drift Lift

**Effective Date:** August 13, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 13, 2026  
**Developer:** Arshiadxt  
**App Name:** Drift Lift  

## 1. Overview
Drift Lift ("the Application") is a desktop gamepad calibration and controller utility developed by Arshiadxt. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

## 2. No Personal Data Collection
Drift Lift does **NOT** collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data, personally identifiable information (PII), telemetry, location data, or usage metrics. 

## 3. Local Data Storage
All application configurations, custom button mappings, calibration profiles, and settings are stored **100% locally** on your device in your user directory (`%APPDATA%\DriftLift` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\DriftLift`). This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server.

## 4. Hardware Interaction
Drift Lift reads local USB and Bluetooth gamepad HID inputs (buttons, triggers, analog stick axes) in real-time solely to provide calibration, visualizer feedback, and virtual controller output. This hardware data is processed transiently in system memory and is never logged or transmitted over a network.

## 5. Third-Party Services & Drivers
- **HidHide Integration:** If you choose to install or launch the optional HidHide driver helper, the Application opens official download links (`https://github.com/nefarius/HidHide`) or launches the local configuration utility (`HidHideClient.exe`). Drift Lift does not share any data with third parties.
- **No Analytics / No Tracking:** Drift Lift contains no analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, advertisement networks, or telemetry services.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Because Drift Lift does not collect any personal data, it complies fully with COPPA and global privacy regulations for all age groups.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted in the repository and reflected in future application releases.
